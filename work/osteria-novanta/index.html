Osteria Novanta

Ninety seats. One long evening.

A neighborhood room for dinner: pasta from the board, a fire for the secondi, and a wine list that stays close to the Alps and the islands. Imagined by Brightverse as a concept, not a restaurant you can book tonight.

This evening

Antipasti

Burrata, late oil, clementine

A cool plate to open the table.

Cicoria and anchovy

Bitter greens, a warm dressing, bread to finish the bowl.

Primi

Tagliatelle al ragù

A slow sauce, a wide ribbon, parmigiano at the table.

Risotto al nero

Cuttlefish, lemon, and a quietly dark plate.

Secondi

Branzino, fennel, capers

The whole fish, for the middle of the table.

Duck, cherries, polenta

A winter plate that stays on the menu when the cherries are gone, as jam.
