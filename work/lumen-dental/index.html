The practice

Built around time, light, and a clear plan.

Lumen is imagined as a small Austin studio: one patient at a time, a short walk from the street, and a team that explains the work in plain language before it begins.

This is a concept design by Brightverse. It is not a working clinic, and no appointments can be booked here.

Restorative work
