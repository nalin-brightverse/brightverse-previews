Denver, Colorado

Built for the weather that built this place.

Roofs held to the snow load, the hail, and the long western sun — specified with care, finished by hand.

The fifth elevation of the house.

A roof is the plane most people never look at and the one the climate never stops testing. Meridian is imagined as a Denver company that treats that plane as architecture: standing seam, cedar, and slate, detailed so the house looks resolved from the ridge down.

Standing seam

Metal roofs cut for the slope, seamed so meltwater has a path and hail has less of a story to tell.

Cedar & shake

Warm roofs for houses that should weather, not fight the years. Installed with ventilation in mind.

Slate & tile

Heavier, quieter work. Chosen when the house is meant to outlast the people who commissioned it.

The approach

Specify first. Climb second.

We walk the attic, the eaves, and the history of leaks before a shingle is ordered. The quote is the scope, written so you can see what is being replaced and what is being left alone.
