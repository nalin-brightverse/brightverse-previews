The atelier

Not a factory of appointments.

Aurelia is imagined as a small Santa Barbara studio: one or two rooms, a short list of treatments, and no language about miracles. Skin, tended. This page is a Brightverse concept design, not a working clinic.

The menu

The consultation

A first conversation and a close look, before anything is applied. The plan is written down.

The facial

A slow, thorough treatment tailored to the skin in front of us — not a numbered package.

Light therapy

Measured sessions for tone and recovery, scheduled with enough space around them to rest.

Skin finish

Peels and polish, used sparingly, and only when the consultation says they belong.

Hands & ritual

A quieter hour for hands, feet, and the kind of care that does not need a before-and-after.

Membership

A simple cadence of visits for those who want the same room, and the same hands, through the year.

By arrangement

Write to the studio.
