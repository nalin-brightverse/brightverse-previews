Hartwell Counsel
Charleston, South Carolina

A private practice

Counsel for matters that should stay quiet.

A small firm for families and closely held companies, written in the tone of a letter, not a billboard.

The docket

Where the work sits.

Closely held companies

Formation, shareholder agreements, and the ordinary disputes that should not become theatre.

Real property

Purchases, easements, and the quiet title work that keeps a house or a lot from becoming a problem.

Estates & gifts

Wills, trusts, and the conversations families would rather have once, and have well.

Private counsel

A standing relationship for people who want advice before they need a filing.

A room in Charleston, and enough time to read the papers.

Hartwell is imagined as a compact practice: a few lawyers, a careful docket, and no claim of being the most aggressive room in the city. This is a Brightverse concept design, not a law firm, and nothing here is legal advice.
